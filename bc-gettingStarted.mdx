<Expandable title="What is Solver's Business Central Connector">
  Solver's Business Central connector is a connector developed and maintained by Solver, enabling Solver customers to connect and load data from your Business Central environment(s) into Solver. The connector utilizes various services provided by Microsoft including 

  - OData Service
  - APIv2 Service

  To connect to any data that is made available in these services. Additionally, Solver creates and maintains an extension that leverages Microsoft's APIv2 service for even easier data integration purposes. 
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How Much Does Solver's Business Central connector cost?">
  The connector is include with your Solver purchase. 
</Expandable>
