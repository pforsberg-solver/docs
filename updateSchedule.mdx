---
title: "Update Schedule"
---

Solver performs monthly maintenance and updates every, 3rd full week Friday of the month. Additionally, as needed, weekly deployments are performed. There are a couple forms of communication that you can follow to keep up to date on updates. 

- Release Notes
- Solver's [Status](https://status.app.solverglobal.com) page
  - All scheduled and unscheduled updates and maintenances notifications are posted here. 
- In-app notifications
  - With every update, Solver posts in app notifications that are displayed on the home page. This notification highlights new features and links to the detailed, release notes.
