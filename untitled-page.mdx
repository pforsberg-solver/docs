# What is Solver?

---

Solver is a complete Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A) solution. Solver all-inclusive pricing gives users, with assigned permissions, to a complete set of tools including Reporting, Planning, Workflows and Data Warehousing; with AI to assist in analysis of your data. 

# What can I use Solver for? 

---

Solver's complete suite of features enables user to 

1. Consolidate you organization's data from many data sources into a single tool. 
2. Learn and leverage one reporting solution to create fully customized, dynamic reports. 
3. Reporting can expand outside of just financial reporting to include operational data. Tie your operation data back to financial data from complete insights. 
4. Take your reports and enabled budgeting. With a couple modifications to your reports, enable these reports to store budget and/or forecasting data. 
5. Process management features such as 
   1. Assignments: Assign budget templates to budgeters with start and end dates. 
   2. Workflows: Incorporate an approval process with you assignments, allowing up to five (5) approavers
   3. Publisher: automated distribution of reports to end users.
