<Expandable title='What does Solver QuickStart give me "right out of the box"? '>
  Solver QuickStart will give you

  1. An integration(s) that will load the prior year and current year to day transactions for a selected module.  
  2. The default dimensions and the option to choose you custom dimension(s) associate to the above selected module.
  3. A scheduled job that will execute once a day, as defined by the user when going through QuickStart. 

  The above integrations and job act as a starting point. You can then expand on this starting point, adding additional data and changing the scheduling to meet your business needs. 
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="When should i use Solver QuickStart">
  As a new implementation, it is best to run Solver QuickStart with you initial kickoff of your implementation. Talk to your Solver Partner and/or implementation consultant for more information. 

  The general rule of thumb is to run Solver QuickStart for at least the General Ledger during your kick off. If Solver QuickStart supports additional modules for your data source, it is recommended to run QuickStart for these modules as well. 
</Expandable>
